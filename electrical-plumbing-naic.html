Good to know

Before you start.

  • List every applianceAircon, ref, washer, water heater, oven: list them so outlets and circuits match what you will actually use.
  • Rough-in before finishesPipes and wires go in before tiles, ceilings and cabinets, so nothing has to be broken open later.
  • Mark where things goTape or mark sink, outlet and switch positions on site, then confirm them with the crew.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you add outlets to a turnover house?

Yes. Added outlets, switches and lighting are common in turnover-house upgrades.

Do you install kitchen sinks?

Yes. Sink counters, water lines and fixtures are part of our kitchen work.

Can electrical and plumbing be part of a bigger renovation?

Yes. That is the usual way: done first, then closed up by ceilings, tiles and cabinets.
