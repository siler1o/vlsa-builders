Good to know

Before you start.

  • Measure after the walls are doneBuilt-ins fit best when they are measured after plastering, tiling and ceiling work.
  • Think about what goes insideLong clothes, shoes, a ref or a microwave: tell us so shelves and spaces match.
  • Pick finishes togetherChoose cabinet colours with your floor and wall finishes so the room looks planned.

Do you make doors?

Yes. Panel doors and jambs have been part of VLSA since the VLSA Woodworks days.

Do you also do gates and grills?

Yes. Gates, window grills and railings are fabricated and installed by our team.
