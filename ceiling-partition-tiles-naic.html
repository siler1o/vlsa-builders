Good to know

Before you start.

  • Plan lights before the ceilingDecide where outlets, downlights and switches go before the ceiling and partitions close up.
  • Tiles before paint and cabinetsTiling is messy. Doing it before paint and built-ins keeps the finished surfaces clean.
  • Buy a few extra tilesKeep a spare box from the same batch for future repairs, since colours vary between batches.

Do you paint exteriors with patterns?
