Good to know

Before you start.

  • Check permits earlyExtensions and structural work usually need a building permit from your municipality. Ask your Office of the Building Official and your homeowners' association before work starts.
  • Fix the roof and leaks firstWater damage ruins new ceilings, paint and cabinets. Roof and waterproofing work should come before interior finishing.
  • Send photos and a rough planPhotos of the area, rough measurements and your developer's floor plan make the first quote faster and closer to the final price.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you build extensions on subdivision townhouses?

Yes. Front extensions with block walls, breeze-block screens and columns are part of our recent work. Check your homeowners' association rules before we start.

Can you do just the roof or just the waterproofing?

Yes. Roofing and waterproofing can be taken as separate jobs.

Do I need a permit?

Extensions and structural changes usually need a building permit from your municipality. Ask your Office of the Building Official before work begins.
