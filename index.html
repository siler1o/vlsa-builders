About VLSA

From woodshop to full builder.

VLSA began as VLSA Woodworks in Brgy. Hulo, Mandaluyong, making doors, cabinets, jambs and all kinds of woodwork.

Today, as VLSA Builders in Brgy. Sabang, Naic, the team takes on full renovation and construction, from ceilings and partitions to roofing and steel fabrication. The woodworker's habit stays: the details you touch and see every day get the most attention.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. VLSA handles minor and major renovation and construction, from a single door or ceiling to a full build.

How do I get a quote?

Send photos of the area, rough measurements and your location through Messenger, the form or a call. We'll reply with next steps.

What areas do you cover?

Our base is Brgy. Sabang, Naic, Cavite. Send us your location and we'll confirm if we can take the job.

Can you make custom doors and cabinets?

Yes. VLSA started as a woodworks shop, so panel doors, jambs, wardrobes, built-ins and beds are made to fit your space.

Are you a registered business?

Yes. VLSA Residential Building Construction is registered with the DTI, Business Name No. 6604577, valid until December 2029.
